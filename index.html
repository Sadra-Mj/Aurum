A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How often are exchange rates updated?+

We check Frankfurter for fresh data when you open this page and every five minutes while it is visible. The underlying reference rates are published daily, rather than continuously. Weekends and holidays may show the last available business day's rate. You can also refresh manually.

Is this the rate I will receive at an exchange?+

These are indicative mid-market reference rates. An exchange provider may apply a spread, commission, or other fees. Always confirm the final rate and total cost with your provider before exchanging money.

Can I exchange or transfer money here?+

This website lets you explore currency rates in Turkish lira. It does not accept deposits, process exchanges, or transfer money. The displayed rates are references, not binding quotes.

What if my connection is interrupted?+

If available, the last successfully loaded rates are saved in your browser and clearly marked as cached. Without saved rates, the site shows an unavailable state instead of invented prices. Use the refresh button to try again.
